# Oglas AI Brand Guidelines

> **Redesign note:** the visual identity (logo, colour palette, typography) is now defined by the 2026 company profile and documented in `DESIGN.md`. The positioning, personality, and voice sections below still apply.

## Brand Position

Oglas AI is a Dubai-based custom software and AI solutions company for businesses that need serious operational systems, not generic technology promises.

The brand should feel:

- Premium, precise, and credible
- Dubai-based but globally relevant
- Technical without sounding cold
- Modern without relying on gimmicky AI visuals
- Business-outcome focused

Primary positioning:

> Custom software and AI systems for companies ready to operate smarter.

Supporting positioning:

> UAE-based, globally available technology partner for ERP, payroll automation, ESS portals, workflow automation, intelligent computer vision, marketing automation, and decision intelligence.

## Brand Personality

Oglas AI should sound like a senior technology partner: calm, sharp, and practical.

Use language that is:

- Confident
- Direct
- Specific
- Operational
- Consultative

Avoid language that is:

- Overhyped
- Vague
- Crypto/Web3-like
- Too playful
- Full of generic “AI transformation” claims

Preferred phrases:

- Custom software
- AI integrated solutions
- Workflow automation
- Operational intelligence
- Built around your business rules
- Designed for real company workflows
- Practical AI for business operations

Avoid phrases:

- Revolutionary AI
- Disrupt your industry
- Next-gen innovation platform
- Unlock limitless potential
- One-size-fits-all solution

## Logo Direction

Current launch mark:

- Black rounded square
- Champagne “O”
- Minimal, premium, monogram-style
- Paired with the wordmark “Oglas AI”

The logo should feel like a luxury technology firm, not a consumer app.

Logo usage:

- Use the full lockup in website headers, footers, proposals, and decks.
- Use the monogram alone for favicons, avatars, app icons, and small UI placements.
- Keep clear space around the mark equal to at least half the icon height.
- Do not add gradients, shadows, bevels, or extra symbols to the logo.

Preferred casing:

**Oglas AI**

Do not use:

- Oglas Ai
- OGLAS AI, except in rare uppercase graphic treatments
- OglasAI

## Color Palette

The palette combines a quiet luxury base with technical accents.

### Primary Colors

| Name | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Onyx | `#080B0B` | Primary text, dark sections, buttons |
| Graphite | `#121716` | Dark surfaces, UI panels |
| Pearl | `#F4F7F5` | Page background, light sections |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Cards, contrast, negative space |

### Accent Colors

| Name | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Champagne | `#C8A96A` | Premium accent, logo, small highlights |
| Emerald | `#0D6A5D` | CTAs, icons, active states |
| Emerald Light | `#65D6BD` | Dark-section status text, subtle highlights |
| Cyan | `#60E6FF` | AI/computer vision cues, technical highlights |

### Neutral Color

| Name | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Steel | `#596461` | Body copy, secondary labels |

## Color Usage Rules

- Use Pearl and White for most page sections.
- Use Onyx for strong contrast sections and primary buttons.
- Use Champagne sparingly. It should feel premium, not decorative.
- Use Cyan only for AI/vision/system-interface moments.
- Use Emerald for trustworthy action, success, and primary icon accents.
- Avoid large full-page gradients.
- Avoid purple/blue SaaS palettes.
- Avoid beige-heavy luxury themes.
- Avoid bright neon backgrounds.

## Typography

### English

Primary font:

**Manrope**

Use it for:

- Headings
- Body copy
- Navigation
- Buttons
- Dashboard-style UI graphics

Typography style:

- Large, confident headings
- Clean line height
- No negative letter spacing
- Avoid overly thin weights

Recommended weights:

- 400 for body
- 500 for navigation and labels
- 600 for headings and buttons
- 700 only for rare emphasis

### Arabic

Recommended Arabic font:

**IBM Plex Sans Arabic**

Arabic guidance:

- Arabic should be written naturally, not directly translated word-for-word.
- Layout should support RTL properly.
- Keep Arabic headings slightly more compact than English if needed.
- Avoid mixing English acronyms into Arabic sentences unless the audience expects them.

## Visual Style

Oglas AI visuals should show systems, dashboards, workflows, and operational intelligence.

Preferred visual motifs:

- Dashboard interfaces
- Workflow health panels
- Camera intelligence grids
- ERP modules
- Approval flows
- Executive reporting views
- Clean system maps
- Subtle grid backgrounds

Avoid:

- Robot faces
- Human brain illustrations
- Abstract glowing orbs
- Random stock business handshakes
- Futuristic city clichés
- Overly dark cyberpunk visuals
- Generic AI circuit backgrounds everywhere

## Layout System

The website should feel like a serious B2B product/service experience.

Use:

- Wide layouts with strong spacing
- Clear section hierarchy
- Grid-based structure
- Sharp 6-8px radius cards
- Minimal borders
- Dense but readable service cards
- Interface-style graphics instead of decorative illustrations

Avoid:

- Nested cards
- Floating decorative sections
- Oversized marketing-only hero sections
- Rounded pill-heavy UI
- Excessive shadows
- Text blocks that explain the UI instead of selling the value

## Buttons

Primary CTA:

**Book a Free Consultation**

Primary button:

- Onyx background
- White text
- 6px border radius
- Optional arrow icon

Secondary button:

- White or Pearl background
- Onyx text
- Subtle border

Button copy should be action-oriented:

- Book a Free Consultation
- Explore Services
- View Service
- Read Insight

Avoid:

- Learn More everywhere
- Get Started, unless used for SaaS/product onboarding
- Submit, except as a form fallback

## Iconography

Use simple line icons, preferably Lucide-style.

Icon rules:

- Use icons to clarify service categories.
- Keep icons small and functional.
- Use Emerald or Champagne for icons.
- Use Cyan only for AI/computer vision/technical signals.
- Do not use large decorative icons as hero artwork.

## Photography and Graphics

For launch, use clean interface-style graphics instead of stock photography.

If photography is introduced later:

- Use real Dubai/business/operations context where possible.
- Keep images bright, sharp, and inspection-friendly.
- Avoid dark, blurred, abstract, or generic stock images.
- Do not show identifiable client facilities without permission.

For case studies:

- Use anonymized interface mockups or abstract operational screenshots.
- Do not expose client logos, names, employee data, financials, or dashboards.

## Case Study Style

Anonymous case studies are appropriate for Oglas AI.

Structure:

1. Anonymous sector name
2. Challenge
3. Solution
4. Outcomes
5. Related services

Examples:

- Manpower Company
- Steel Manufacturing Company
- UAE-based Staffing Group
- Industrial Operations Firm

Do not include:

- Client names
- Client logos
- Exact internal numbers unless approved
- Screenshots with sensitive data
- Any confidential workflow details

Good:

## Bilingual Guidance

Launch approach:

- English first
- Arabic structure ready
- Arabic content added after final English approval

Arabic version should:

- Use RTL layout
- Preserve the premium tone
- Localize business meaning, not just translate words
- Keep English service acronyms where common, such as ERP, CRM, AI

## Digital Application

Website:

- Light background with premium dark interface visuals
- Clear CTAs
- Fast-loading pages
- No visual clutter

Presentations:

- Use dark title slides with Champagne accent
- Use white content slides with Onyx headings
- Show workflows and dashboards as proof

Proposals:

- Lead with business problem and proposed system
- Use diagrams and service modules
- Keep pricing custom quote only

Social:

- Use short practical insights
- Avoid motivational business quotes
- Focus on automation, ERP, AI, and operations

## Do and Don't

Do:

- Be specific about business workflows.
- Show interface-style graphics.
- Keep client information confidential.
- Use Champagne sparingly.
- Sound calm and credible.
- Emphasize UAE base and global availability.

Don't:

- Overpromise AI results.
- Use generic technology buzzwords.
- Mention confidential clients.
- Use cartoon AI visuals.
- Turn the brand into a one-color palette.
- Make every page feel like a landing page.

## Brand Summary

Oglas AI should feel like a premium Dubai technology partner that understands real business operations and builds custom software with practical AI where it creates measurable value.

The brand is not about AI hype. It is about better systems, cleaner workflows, smarter visibility, and software built around how companies actually work.
