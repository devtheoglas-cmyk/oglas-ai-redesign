# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Business owners, managers, and operations leaders — primarily in Dubai and the wider UAE, also globally — who have a workflow that is too manual, systems that don't connect, or an idea for using AI, and who are deciding whether Oglas AI is the right partner to build it. (Inferred from the site copy and company profile; owner confirmed the site content stays as-is.)

## Product Purpose
Marketing site for Oglas AI, a Dubai-based custom software and AI solutions company. It explains services (custom software, workflow automation, AI dashboards, computer vision, ERP/payroll, etc.), industries served, case studies, insights, and converts visitors into consultation requests through the contact form.

## Positioning
Problem-first, not product-first: Oglas AI understands how a business actually operates before choosing technology, then builds custom software and practical AI around it.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Next.js 16 App Router + Tailwind v4, Sanity for insights, Resend for the contact form.
- This folder is a separate redesign copy of the live oglasai.com repo (`devtheoglas-cmyk/oglas-ai-web`, main branch). The live site and its repo must not be touched.
- Content is fixed: every page's copy, links, SEO metadata, structured data, and contact details stay exactly as on the live site. Only the design changes.
- Stays local for now; nothing is published or pushed until the owner says so.

## Evidence on Hand

## Product Principles
- Business before technology — every section should read as practical, not hype.
- Clarity over spectacle on long service pages; spectacle belongs in heroes and feature bands.
- Never alter or invent content in the name of design.
